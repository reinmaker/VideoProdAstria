Four concepts to know. First — workspaces. A workspace is a brand or project with its own product catalog. Second — references. A reference is a trained model of a specific product, added to your prompt with the at symbol. References are what make Astria different — you're pointing to the exact item, not describing it in words. Third — prompts. A prompt describes the scene you want; the references bring the products into it. And fourth — packs. A pack bundles a prompt, references, and settings into one saved setup for a complete brand workflow.
